# Crea8iv Media Website Enhancement Plan

Date: 2026-07-13  
Website: https://crea8ivmedia.com/  
Purpose: Upgrade Crea8iv Media's website using the FTZ Digital competitor analysis while strengthening Crea8iv's own positioning, SEO, proof, and lead-generation flow.

## Strategic Direction

Crea8iv Media should not copy FTZ Digital's generic full-service agency positioning. Crea8iv should own a sharper market position:

**Crea8iv Media is a Rawalpindi and Islamabad growth systems agency that connects Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Business Profile, SEO, websites, content, automation, and reporting into one lead-generation engine.**

This is stronger than saying "digital marketing agency" alone because it gives buyers a clear outcome: predictable leads, better visibility, and measurable growth.

## Website Goals

1. Increase qualified inquiries from Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Bahria Town, DHA, and Pakistan-wide businesses.
2. Make every service page more conversion-focused with proof, process, deliverables, FAQs, and clear CTAs.
3. Turn Crea8iv's real client work into visible portfolio and case-study assets.
4. Improve local SEO, AI-search visibility, and internal linking.
5. Reduce lead friction with WhatsApp, contact forms, maps, and consultation CTAs.

## Site-Wide Conversion Upgrades

### 1. Floating WhatsApp CTA

Add a WhatsApp bubble across the site:

- Button text bubble: `Chat with Crea8iv Media`
- Microcopy: `Usually replies within business hours`
- Prefilled message: `Hi Crea8iv Media, I want to grow my business. Can you guide me?`

Why: FTZ reduces friction well with WhatsApp. In Pakistan's local service market, WhatsApp often converts better than form-only contact.

### 2. Proof Strip On Every Core Page

Add a compact proof row near the top of homepage and service pages:

- `Rawalpindi & Islamabad based`
- `Meta Ads + Google Ads + GBP systems`
- `Clinics, salons, restaurants, real estate, ecommerce`
- `Weekly reporting`
- `Free strategy audit`

Use real numbers only where verified.

### 3. Better Lead Capture

Update contact forms with:

- Full name
- Phone / WhatsApp
- Business name
- City
- Service interested in
- Monthly marketing budget range
- Main goal
- Hidden UTM fields

Add service routing so inquiries can be tagged as Meta Ads, Google Ads, GBP, Website, Social Media, Content, Branding, Course, or Consultation.

### 4. Schema Markup

Add these schema types:

| Page Type | Schema |
| --- | --- |
| Home | LocalBusiness, Organization, WebSite |
| About / Team | Organization, Person |
| Service pages | Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList |
| Pricing | Offer, PriceSpecification, FAQPage |
| Results / Case Studies | CreativeWork, Article, BreadcrumbList |
| Blog posts | Article, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage where relevant |
| Contact | LocalBusiness, PostalAddress, GeoCoordinates |

## Final Recommendation

The best version of Crea8iv's website should feel more specific than FTZ, more proof-driven than a typical local agency, and more connected than a portfolio site. Every page should answer three questions quickly:

3. What is the easiest next step to talk to them?
