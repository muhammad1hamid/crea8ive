# Homepage Soft-Orange Refinement

## Implemented

- Replaced blue-grey canvases with neutral charcoal and warm-grey text. Pink, blue and purple interface accents are softened to peach and champagne; meaningful teal/green accents and authentic client imagery remain.
- Softened homepage hero and header actions to a light-orange finish. Added a restrained navigation reflection while preserving logo assets, dimensions and the hero composition.
- Website projects use three equal-width desktop cards with consistent 4:3 media frames. Absolutely positioned, contained images cannot resize the frame; copy and actions align along the shared card bottom. Tablet and mobile retain horizontal carousel navigation.
- Rebuilt growth services as eight icon-led cards instead of inconsistent poster visuals. Service titles, descriptions, links and desktop/mobile accessibility remain.
- Retained gentle glossy reflections on case-study cards. Creative evidence now uses consistent 4:5 frames, with contain fitting and the existing enlarged image viewer. The growth-tool illustration uses the warmer palette.
