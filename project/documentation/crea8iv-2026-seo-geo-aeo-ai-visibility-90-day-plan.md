# Crea8iv Media 2026 SEO, GEO, AEO, and AI Visibility Plan

Date: 2026-08-14
Website: https://crea8ivmedia.com
Primary markets: Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Pakistan
Primary goal: Maximize qualified organic visibility, AI answer visibility, local search presence, and conversion from non-paid discovery.

## Strategic Position

2026 search visibility is one connected system:

1. Technical SEO makes the site crawlable, fast, indexable, and usable.
2. Entity SEO makes Crea8iv Media, Syed Qamar Abbas, services, locations, and proof understandable.
3. AEO makes pages answer buyer questions directly.
4. GEO makes content citable by AI systems through evidence, structure, freshness, and third-party corroboration.
5. Conversion tracking proves which visibility actually creates leads.

This plan follows current Google Search guidance: AI Overviews and AI Mode still depend on core Search systems, crawlable helpful content, technical clarity, and page experience. Google also says there is no special schema or llms.txt requirement for Google AI features; llms.txt is optional for other AI systems and agents.

Reference guidance:

- Google AI features and your website: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- Google AI optimization guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- Google Search Generative AI performance reports: https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports
- Google 2026 Search updates, including FAQ rich result removal: https://developers.google.com/search/updates

## Workstreams

### 1. Technical Performance and Crawlability

Goal: Remove the mobile performance gap and make every important page easy to crawl.

Actions:

- Keep homepage LCP under 2.5 seconds on mobile.
- Compress and serve all large visual assets from optimized WebP/AVIF variants.
- Lazy-load below-fold images and non-critical widgets.
- Keep third-party tracking lazy-loaded unless legally or operationally required early.
- Confirm sitemap includes homepage, service pages, blog posts, tool pages, case studies, author page, founder page, RSS.
- Confirm robots.txt does not block Googlebot, Bingbot, or important assets.
- Confirm canonical URLs use `https://crea8ivmedia.com`.
- Validate no old Astro output is deployed.

Deliverables:

- Core Web Vitals benchmark report.
- Image payload report.
- Crawl/indexation checklist.
- Search Console and Bing Webmaster baseline.

### 3. AEO and GEO Content Format

Each important page should include:

- A direct answer in the first 100 words.
- One clear H1.
- H2 sections phrased like real user questions.
- Comparison tables where buyers compare options.
- Step-by-step sections for process queries.
- Source-backed statistics.
- Author/founder attribution.
- Last updated date.
- Internal links to service, tool, case study, and relevant blog pages.

Reusable content blocks:

1. Definition block: "What is X?"
2. Cost block: "How much does X cost in Pakistan?"
3. Comparison block: "Google Ads vs SEO", "Meta Ads vs Google Ads"
4. Local intent block: "Best option for Rawalpindi/Islamabad businesses"
5. Tool CTA block: "Calculate your budget/check your visibility"
6. Proof block: case example, screenshots, campaign outcome, client-safe result
7. Expert note: short quote or note from Syed Qamar Abbas
