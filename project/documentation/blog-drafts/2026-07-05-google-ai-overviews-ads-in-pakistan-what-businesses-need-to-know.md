# Google AI Overviews Are Now Showing Ads in Pakistan: What Every Business Needs to Know

Google search just changed in a way that affects every business in Pakistan spending money on SEO or Google Ads.

AI Overviews — the AI-generated answer panels that appear at the very top of Google results — are now live with paid advertisements embedded directly inside them. Pakistan is included. This is not a future development. It is happening right now, and your competitors are either adapting or falling behind.

Here is what this actually means for businesses in Rawalpindi, Islamabad, and across Pakistan.

## What Are Google AI Overviews?

When someone searches Google for something like "best dentist in Rawalpindi" or "how much does a website cost in Pakistan," Google now often shows an AI-generated summary at the top of the results before any websites, maps, or ads.

This summary is built using information from multiple sources. Google's AI reads and synthesizes content from around the web, then writes a direct answer.

The result: millions of people get their answer without ever clicking on a website.

This is called the zero-click problem, and it has arrived in Pakistan at scale.

Google's current Ads Help documentation explicitly lists Pakistan among the countries where ads in AI Overviews are available in English on mobile and desktop, which removes the guesswork for local advertisers planning around this change.

## Ads Are Now Inside the AI Overview

The bigger development for businesses running paid campaigns is this: Google has begun placing ads directly inside AI Overview panels in Pakistan.

That means:

- A user searches for "digital marketing agency in Rawalpindi"
- Google shows an AI-generated summary of options and what to look for
- Inside or around that summary, ads appear that the user sees before any organic result
- A user can click directly from the AI panel to a business without ever going to the traditional search results page

This is premium real estate. And it is expensive. But it is also where the highest-intent clicks now happen.

## Why Zero-Click Search Matters More Than Most Businesses Realize

Here is the number that should get your attention:

In 2026, an estimated **65% to 69% of Google searches end without a single click to any website**.

That means more than half of the people searching for your services read what Google shows them, get their answer, and move on — without visiting anyone's website.

For businesses that built their entire strategy around "rank on page one and get clicks," this is a real problem.

For businesses that adapt, it is an opportunity to be the source Google cites.

## The New Priority: Being Cited, Not Just Ranked

The goal of SEO is shifting.

In the old model, you tried to rank number one so people would click your link.

In the new model, you try to be the source that Google's AI cites inside the AI Overview — so even when no one clicks, your brand name and expertise appear at the top of every answer.

This is what the industry now calls **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** or **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)**.

It requires different content strategy:

- Short, direct, quotable answers to specific questions
- Evidence blocks that AI systems can read and verify
- Structured schema markup that helps Google understand your expertise
- Consistent business entity signals across your website, GBP, and third-party sources

## FAQ

### What are Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews are AI-generated answer panels that appear at the top of Google search results. They synthesize information from multiple sources and answer the user's query directly on the search results page, reducing the need to click through to any website.

### Are Google AI Overviews live in Pakistan?

Yes. Google AI Overviews are now active in Pakistan on both mobile and desktop search results for a growing range of queries including commercial and local service searches.

### Can ads appear inside Google AI Overviews in Pakistan?

Yes. Google has expanded the Ads in AI Overviews format to Pakistan. Businesses running Search, Performance Max, and Shopping campaigns are eligible to serve ads within or adjacent to AI Overview panels.

### What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization. Traditional SEO focuses on ranking web pages in organic results. GEO focuses on making your content the source that AI systems cite inside AI Overviews and answer engine responses. Both matter in 2026, but GEO is the newer, higher-priority layer for businesses with strong informational content.

### How can a business in Rawalpindi or Islamabad benefit from AI Overviews?

Local businesses can earn citations inside AI Overviews by optimizing their Google Business Profile, publishing direct and expert content about local services, using FAQPage and LocalBusiness schema, and building consistent entity signals. These actions increase the chance that Google's AI will cite and feature your business when a local user searches for your services.

### Does being cited in an AI Overview replace traditional SEO?

No. Traditional SEO still drives traffic from users who scroll past the AI Overview to organic results or maps. GEO and traditional SEO are complementary. The businesses with the strongest position are investing in both simultaneously.
