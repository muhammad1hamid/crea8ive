## Common Mistakes To Avoid

Do not treat this work as a one-time design review. The site will change as campaigns, tracking scripts, services, pricing, seasonal offers, and customer expectations change. Every new script, image, form, or content block can improve the page or slow it down.

Do not judge the experience only from a large desktop monitor. Review the same path on a phone, on a real connection, with the same level of patience a new customer would have. The most honest audit is often done away from the design tool.

Do not separate SEO from user experience. Search systems increasingly reward pages that are clear, useful, technically accessible, and semantically well connected. A page that answers real questions in a structured way is better for users, Google, and AI answer engines.

## FAQ

### Are Core Web Vitals a ranking factor?

Core Web Vitals are part of Google's page experience signals. They are not the only ranking factor, but they matter because they affect user experience and can influence performance in competitive search results.

### What are good Core Web Vitals scores?

Good thresholds are LCP at or below 2.5 seconds, INP at or below 200 milliseconds, and CLS at or below 0.1 for most real-user visits.

### Should I optimize the homepage first?

Start with the pages that affect revenue or search visibility most: landing pages, service pages, product pages, article templates, forms, and checkout or booking paths.

### Can plugins hurt Core Web Vitals?

Yes. Sliders, chat widgets, tracking scripts, page builders, review widgets, and unused plugin assets can slow loading, delay interaction, or shift layout.
