## Common Mistakes To Avoid

Do not treat this work as a one-time design review. The site will change as campaigns, tracking scripts, services, pricing, seasonal offers, and customer expectations change. Every new script, image, form, or content block can improve the page or slow it down.

Do not judge the experience only from a large desktop monitor. Review the same path on a phone, on a real connection, with the same level of patience a new customer would have. The most honest audit is often done away from the design tool.

Do not separate SEO from user experience. Search systems increasingly reward pages that are clear, useful, technically accessible, and semantically well connected. A page that answers real questions in a structured way is better for users, Google, and AI answer engines.

## FAQ

### What is website speed optimization?

Website speed optimization is the process of making pages load, respond, and remain stable faster by improving assets, code, hosting, caching, and user-facing templates.

### What usually slows a website down?

Common causes include oversized images, heavy JavaScript, too many third-party scripts, slow hosting, page builders, unoptimized fonts, render-blocking resources, and unstable embedded content.

### Does page speed affect conversions?

Yes. Slow or unstable pages interrupt trust and momentum, especially on mobile and paid traffic journeys where visitors compare options quickly.

### Should I chase a perfect PageSpeed score?

No. Use speed tools to diagnose problems, but prioritize improvements that make important pages faster and easier for real users to complete.
