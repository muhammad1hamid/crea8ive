### ProPakistani article outline

Working title: **Before Your Business Deploys AI, Answer These Five Governance Questions**

Target reader: Pakistan-based founders and managers of service businesses adopting AI for lead capture, support, marketing, or internal operations.

Length: 900 to 1,100 words.

1. The adoption mistake: treating AI as a tool purchase
   Explain why customer-facing automation changes how a company handles information, decisions, and accountability.
2. Who owns the source of truth?
   Identify the business facts AI systems need: prices, services, location, policies, availability, and approved claims.
3. What can the system say, and what must it never invent?
   Set rules for availability, medical or legal claims, pricing, offers, promises, and references to competitors.
4. When must a human take over?
   Define escalation for complaints, payments, sensitive information, complex quotes, and high-risk advice.
5. How will the business protect customer information?
   Cover access controls, data retention, vendors, permissions, and the need to avoid putting unnecessary data into tools.
6. How will the business measure whether it helps?
   Use operational measures: accurate answers, qualified leads, response time, escalations, resolved issues, and customer feedback.
7. A simple 30-day implementation sequence
   Audit business facts, document guardrails, test with real questions, assign an owner, then deploy in a narrow workflow.
