# Qatar research protocol — unpublished

Status: methodology prepared; no data or findings collected.

Studies: AI Visibility Index; Local Search Benchmark; Website Performance Report; WhatsApp Lead Response Study; Clinic Visibility; Real Estate Visibility; Restaurant Maps Benchmark.

For each study record the research question, inclusion/exclusion criteria, sampling frame, sample size, collection dates, platform/mode, language, location settings, consent basis, source URL and evidence capture. Choose the sample before observing results. Keep missing data and failed attempts visible.

Response studies require participating-business consent and a controlled test plan. Do not submit unsolicited fake customer enquiries. Website measurements need a fixed device/network profile, tool version and repeated observations. AI observations need exact prompts and session context; report mentions and citations separately.

Before publication: check data provenance, deduplicate records, reproduce calculations, review outliers and limitations, obtain needed permissions, and name the actual reviewer. Publish the method and sample coverage alongside findings. Do not fabricate a benchmark or fill empty observations with estimated results.
