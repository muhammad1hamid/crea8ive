# Qatar release and 90-day operating plan

Prepared 20 September 2026. The website implementation is local; this schedule starts after an approved release. Dates and outcomes are planning assumptions, not commitments or forecasts.

## Release preparation

- Business owner: approve current official contact routes, scope-only QAR packages, and English copy. Keep Qatar status `prelaunch` until documentary registration facts are verified.
- Arabic editor: review all 15 priority pages, labels, and query hypotheses in `arabic-keyword-map.csv`. Search volume and demand remain unmeasured.
- Production lead: complete `production-rights-register.csv`; include client, commissioning party, exact individual role, credit line, permission evidence and allowed media. Do not publish named work before this review.
- Hosting owner: choose the existing Apache/PHP path or explicitly configure the Node-to-PHP bridge. Confirm private durable storage, backups, access control, staff access to enquiries and an operational response owner. Form saving alone does not send email or confirm an appointment.
- Release owner: deploy only after approval, retain rollback artifact, check real server redirects and status codes, rerun the route verifier, check a real authorized test enquiry, then submit the sitemap in the existing Search Console property.

## Days 1–30: establish the baseline

Release the core English and Arabic journeys together after editorial review. Review actual query impressions, landing-page engagement and qualified enquiries weekly. Verify GA4 events in DebugView on the deployed site before designating successful `qatar_form_submit` as a key event. Click events are intent signals, not saved leads or confirmed appointments. Do not report a local dataLayer check as live GA4 receipt.

Record the 15 target prompts across relevant platforms, language and search mode with evidence. Use a consistent schedule and record failures/non-answers too. Add genuine observations to `observations.csv`, then run `python3 scripts/qatar_visibility_report.py`. The dashboard starts explicitly unmeasured.

Read and approve the first six practical guides; use real customer questions to expand useful sections. A short guide is not a comprehensive market report. No numeric 2027 cost or ranking claims are currently made.

## Days 31–60: deepen proof and follow-up

Review the remaining articles in small editorial batches. Add permission-cleared production examples and real project scope; publish client results only with a source, date range and release approval. Evaluate actual inquiry quality by service and language. Assign a response owner and agreed business hours; measure first human response and lost handoffs before automating.

Use the research protocol for one narrow, reproducible pilot. Keep raw data and evidence private. Publish methodology and limitations alongside any eventual findings. Add another location page only when it answers a different local need with evidence.

## Days 61–90: refine from evidence

Compare like-for-like prompt observations and actual conversion cohorts; avoid claiming causation from a single AI answer. Improve pages with useful intent evidence and customer feedback. Review offers and QAR scope economics with the owner. Add software demos and working automation only where delivery capacity, consent and integration access are approved.
