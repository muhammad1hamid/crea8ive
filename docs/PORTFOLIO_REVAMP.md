Owner request, 10 November 2026. Agency service portfolio at `/results/`.

- One fullscreen scroll section, small single-line “Our Work” heading in the approved Josefin Sans typography. Homepage cream/forest/charcoal and orange accents remain the source of truth.
- This page has explicit permission for perspective-like depth, alternating left/right entrances, blur-to-clear movement and hover reveals. This is a page-specific exception to the default exclusion of carousel/perspective cards.
- Original project artwork and content registries supply the collection. Resolve case study and live destinations independently; never fabricate missing links. Concepts remain labelled.
- Native scrolling drives a sticky stage. Foreground cards are interactive; distant cards are inert. Previous/next controls and a searchable, filterable “View all” mode make the archive reachable without precision scrolling.

- Hover, keyboard focus or a tap opens the foreground title, industry and actions over the lower part of the image. Distant prints show only artwork. Use the recorded live and case-study links where available and the project's own preview where a destination is missing. The static collection keeps titles and links visible for reduced motion and short or narrow screens.
- Only `/results/` hides the shared footer, progress strip and floating widgets. A corner button toggles the existing navbar. Other pages retain their footer and widgets.
- The shared navbar hides on downward scroll and holds that state when scrolling stops. It reappears on upward scroll or near the top. Menu use and keyboard focus keep it accessible.
- No new dependencies, invented client results, generated client logos, or homepage hero changes.
