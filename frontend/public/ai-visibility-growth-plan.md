## Purpose

This file summarizes the AI visibility and organic traffic growth operating plan for Crea8iv Media. It is designed for AI assistants, search systems, operators and buyers who need a parseable overview of how Crea8iv Media tracks AI citations, improves content visibility and converts traffic into leads.

## Core objective

Crea8iv Media should improve AI visibility and organic traffic by making its public information clear, factual, structured, internally linked, proof-backed and easy to cite. The goal is not to manipulate AI systems. The goal is to make the company, founder, services, locations, proof, pricing and contact path easier to understand accurately.

## Weekly monitoring workflow

1. Test each priority query in Google Search, visible Google AI results, ChatGPT with search, Perplexity and Gemini.
2. Log whether Crea8iv Media is cited, mentioned, absent or described incorrectly.
3. Record which page was cited, which competitor was cited and whether the answer used current facts.
4. Check Search Console impressions, clicks and average position for the same query cluster.
5. Review WhatsApp clicks, call clicks, form submits, tool completions and consultation visits from related pages.

## Monthly optimization workflow

1. Improve service pages with direct answer blocks for fit, scope, pricing, timeline, proof and next step.
2. Add or refresh FAQ content only where the answers are visible on the page.
3. Connect every priority service page to a tool, answer brief, proof page and consultation path.
4. Update machine-readable files when services, pricing, proof, URLs or positioning changes.
5. Publish one supporting guide, comparison, playbook, answer brief or proof update for the weakest query cluster.

## 90-day rollout

### Weeks 1-2: Measurement and entity cleanup

- Create a query tracking sheet for AI mentions, citations, competitors and source URLs.
- Confirm consistency across the website, Google Business Profile, social profiles, founder page and machine-readable files.
- Add missing SameAs, proof and source-of-truth links only where they are real and public.

### Weeks 3-4: Top service page upgrades

- Refresh the top eight service pages with direct answer blocks.
- Connect every priority service page to one tool, one answer brief, one proof page and one consultation path.
- Validate that FAQ schema matches visible page content.

### Month 2: Authority content sprint

- Publish high-intent guides around AI visibility, local SEO, website development, clinics, restaurants, ads and automation.
- Create comparison pages where buyers naturally ask X vs Y questions.
- Add one portfolio or proof update for each priority industry cluster when real evidence is available.

### Month 3: Distribution and citation building

- Repurpose guides into founder LinkedIn posts, Google Business Profile posts, YouTube scripts and short answer assets.
- Build consistent third-party mentions without fake awards, fake metrics or unsupported claims.
- Review Search Console and AI mention logs to decide the next content cluster.
