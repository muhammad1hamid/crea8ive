## Purpose

This roadmap lists the priority content assets Crea8iv Media should publish or refresh to increase search visibility, AI mentions, source citations and qualified leads.

## Publishing principles

- Lead each page with a direct 40-60 word answer block.
- Use comparison tables for X vs Y buyer questions.
- Use numbered lists for process and how-to content.
- Link each asset to one service page, one tool, one proof page and one consultation path.
- Keep claims accurate and visible on the page.
- Do not use fake proof, fake metrics, fake reviews, unsupported awards or unsupported credentials.
- Show last updated date and author/entity context.
- Track Search Console impressions, clicks, AI citations and lead actions after publishing.
